## Task 0.5 — Fake data generator

- **Machine:** VPS
- **Stop for review:** **yes** (end of Phase 0)

Write `api/scripts/seed.py`.

### Data
- 1 owner (`owner` / `owner123`) and 4 salespeople (`sales1`..`sales4` / `sales123`). Dev only; say so in README.
- About 40 products: aircons (3–4 brands, 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 HP, inverter and non-inverter, `warranty_months`
  12 or 24 or 36), accessories (bracket, copper pipe, remote, stabilizer, drain pipe, gas refill), services
  (installation, cleaning, repair). Realistic MMK prices (aircons ~900,000–3,500,000).
- About 60 customers; about 30% of phones missing; two customers share one phone (D15).
  About 40% of sales are walk-in (no customer); walk-ins mostly buy accessories or services.
- 90 days of sales ending **today in Asia/Yangon**, during opening hours (09:00–19:00 Yangon).
  Hot months (Mar–May) have more aircon sales; aircon sales usually include installation and accessories.
- Payment: ~85% paid, ~10% partial, ~5% unpaid. Methods mixed across cash/bank/kbzpay/wavepay/other;
  none on unpaid invoices.
- Every aircon line has one serial per unit; `warranty_until` from the product's `warranty_months` (D14).
- About 2% of sales void. Use the **void procedure** from `schema.md` (serials moved to `voided_sale_serials`).
  At least one voided serial is re-sold on a later sale (proves D13 works).
- Invoice numbers from `invoice_no_seq` in `sold_at` order.
- Fixed random seed: the same data every run.

### Rules
- `--reset` empties our tables and resets sequences, then seeds.
- With `APP_ENV=production`, `--reset` refuses and exits non-zero **before touching the database**.
- Prints row counts per table and the total of completed sales at the end.

### Done when
- `python scripts/seed.py --reset` runs without errors and prints row counts.
- Running it twice prints identical counts and totals.
- pytest (against `aircon_test`):
  - `APP_ENV=production` + `--reset` exits non-zero and the data is untouched;
  - every sale's `total_amount` = sum of its `sale_items.line_total`;
  - every aircon line has exactly `quantity` serials; non-aircon lines have none;
  - every void sale has its serials only in `voided_sale_serials`, none in `sale_item_serials`;
  - no sale outside 09:00–19:00 Yangon; the last sale day equals today in Yangon.
- `pytest` passes (all tests, including 0.4's).
- Report written, committed, pushed.
